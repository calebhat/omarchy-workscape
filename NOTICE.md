WorkScape is a fork of [tenzin.auto-workspace](https://github.com/yesheytenzin/auto-workspace)
(MIT). Original launch-on-workspace UI and helper script are from that project.

This fork adds monitor-layout profiles, EDID matching, workspace-to-monitor
pins, network-aware profile select, overflow/Stage chain, locked pane sizes,
trackpad gesture profiles, and bar/login apply.

Named split shapes, divider snapping, and the live bar miniature follow the
approach in [bjarneo/omarchy-workspace-layout](https://github.com/bjarneo/omarchy-workspace-layout)
(MIT). WorkScape still stamps window geometry rather than registering a Lua
tiling layout.
